# Ghost Agent: market size, AI data-leak behaviour and Nordic adoption (research as of 8 Nov 2026)

Scope: hard numbers on (1) AI-data-security / GenAI-DLP / DLP market size, (2) evidence that people paste sensitive data into AI chat tools, (3) AI-chat adoption among Norwegian/Nordic SMBs, public sector, individuals, teachers and health workers, (4) breach/fine statistics relevant to small organisations, (5) counts of reachable small organisations in Norway.

Method note: web research only (search + page fetch). Many figures come from press coverage of vendor reports, not the PDFs; where I fetched the primary page this is marked "(primary)". Vendor telemetry reports (Cyberhaven, Harmonic, LayerX, Netskope, Menlo) are written by companies that sell AI-DLP. Their samples are enterprise customers, mostly US/UK, and their definitions of "sensitive" differ. Market-research numbers (MarketsandMarkets, Grand View, IMARC and others) are paywalled estimates with opaque methods. They disagree with each other by 2–5x for the same segment.

## 1. Market size and growth: AI data security, GenAI DLP, DLP; SMB share

### Takeaway
No analyst publishes a "GenAI DLP for SMB" segment. The nearest segments are AI TRiSM, put at about USD 2.3–2.8B in 2024/25 growing about 22% a year (Grand View), and "generative AI cybersecurity", put at about USD 7–8.7B in 2024/25 growing 26–33% a year (MarketsandMarkets). The latter is much broader: it includes AI used *for* security. Classic DLP is put at about USD 2.6–3.1B in 2025. Every source says large enterprises dominate DLP spend. I found no published SMB share.

### Cited Findings
- **Generative AI cybersecurity (MarketsandMarkets, Aug 2025 edition):** USD 8.65B in 2025, rising to USD 35.50B by 2031, CAGR 26.5% — [MarketsandMarkets press release via ContentEngine, 25 Aug 2025](https://contentenginellc.com/2025/08/25/generative-ai-cybersecurity-market-worth-3550-billion-by-2031-marketsandmarketstm)
- **Earlier MarketsandMarkets edition (2024):** USD 7.1B in 2024, rising to USD 40.1B by 2030, CAGR 33.4%. The two editions are not consistent with each other — [MarketsandMarkets press release](https://marketsandmarkets.com/PressReleases/generative-ai-cybersecurity.asp); [PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/generative-ai-cybersecurity-market-worth-40-1-billion-by-2030---exclusive-report-by-marketsandmarkets-302207359.html)
- **PS Market Research:** USD 7.1B in 2024, rising to about USD 40.0B by 2030 (CAGR 33.7%) — [PS Market Research](https://www.psmarketresearch.com/market-analysis/generative-ai-cybersecurity-market/market-size)
- **Precedence Research (much lower):** "generative AI in cybersecurity" at USD 2B in 2024, rising to about USD 14.79B by 2034. This shows how much the segment definition varies — [mojoauth summary citing Precedence](https://mojoauth.com/blog/generative-ai-cybersecurity-market-trends-business)
- **AI TRiSM (trust, risk and security management; Grand View Research, 2025):** USD 7.44B by 2030, CAGR 21.6% for 2025–2030. Grand View's scope table lists USD 2.80B for 2025. A syndicated release gives USD 2.34B for 2024. The solution segment held about 70% of 2024 revenue. The report is segmented "by enterprise size", but no SME figure is public — [GlobeNewswire, 21 May 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/05/21/3085951/28124/en/AI-Trust-Risk-and-Security-Management-Trends-Analysis-and-Growth-Forecasts-2025-2030-Market-to-Reach-7-44-Billion-Generative-AI-Foundation-Models-Spur-Demand-for-Advanced-AI-TRiSM-.html); [GII listing of the Grand View report](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/grvi1726337-ai-trust-risk-security-management-market-size.html)
- **AI prompt security (narrowest segment; The Business Research Company):** USD 1.98B in 2025, rising to USD 5.87B by 2029, CAGR 31.2% — [GlobeNewswire, 7 Jan 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/07/3214674/0/en/p.html)
- **AI in cybersecurity (broad, mostly AI *for* defence):** USD 34.1B in 2025, rising to USD 234.3B by 2032, CAGR 31.7% — [Persistence Market Research](https://www.persistencemarketresearch.com/market-research/artificial-intelligence-ai-in-cybersecurity-market.asp). Another publisher gives USD 28.51B in 2025, rising to USD 136.18B by 2032 (CAGR 24.81%) — [GlobeNewswire, 23 Oct 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/10/23/3171810/28124/en/Artificial-Intelligence-in-Cybersecurity-Market-Report-2025.html)
- **Classic DLP market:** IMARC puts it at USD 3.1B in 2025, and other publishers at about USD 2.7B in 2025 or USD 2.58B in 2024 — [IMARC via GII](https://www.gii.tw/report/imarc1941601-data-loss-prevention-market-size-share-trends.html). Grand View (2023 release) forecast USD 9.33B by 2030; its scope is far wider — [PR Newswire UK, Grand View, 2023](https://prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/data-loss-prevention-market-to-be-worth-9-33-billion-by-2030-grand-view-research-inc-301878317.html)
- **SMB share of DLP:** Grand View's 2026–2033 DLP report has an "enterprise size" chart with an SME section, but the values are not public — [Grand View TOC](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/data-loss-prevention-market/toc). Market Research Forecast states that the large-enterprise segment leads the DLP tools market because large firms hold more sensitive data — [Market Research Forecast](https://www.marketresearchforecast.com/reports/data-loss-prevention-tools-25494)
- **Analyst demand signal:** Gartner (17 Feb 2025) predicts that by 2027 more than 40% of AI-related data breaches will be caused by improper cross-border use of GenAI, and that "adoption by end-users has outpaced the development of data governance and security measures". This is a prediction, not an observation — [Gartner press release, 17 Feb 2025](https://gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-02-17-gartner-predicts-forty-percent-of-ai-data-breaches-will-arise-from-cross-border-genai-misuse-by-2027)
- **How many organisations already have GenAI DLP:** among Netskope's enterprise customers, 50% use DLP for genAI and 50% lack enforceable data-protection policies for genAI apps (Oct 2024–Oct 2025 data) — [Netskope Cloud & Threat Report 2026, 5 Jan 2026 (primary)](https://threatlabs.netskope.com/reports/2026/cloud-and-threat-report-2026)

### Inferences
- The figures nearest to "Ghost Agent's market" are AI prompt security (about USD 2B, 2025) and AI TRiSM (about USD 2.3–2.8B, 2024/25). Even these are enterprise-weighted. "Generative AI cybersecurity" (about USD 8.65B) mostly counts AI-powered security tooling and should not be used as the TAM (total addressable market).
- DLP grows at single to low double digits, while the AI-security segments grow 20–33% a year. The growth is in the AI segments.
- If even large enterprises with security teams are only 50% covered by GenAI DLP (Netskope), coverage among Nordic micro and small organisations is very likely close to zero. This is an inference; no SMB coverage data exists.
- A bottom-up TAM (organisation counts × price) will be more defensible for the SMB/Nordic segment than any top-down analyst figure.

### Gaps
- No public SMB or SME revenue share for DLP, AI TRiSM or GenAI security. The figures sit behind paywalls (Grand View, MarketsandMarkets).
- No Nordic or Norway-specific market sizing for AI data security found.
- I could not find a MarketsandMarkets "AI data security" or "AI data protection" market value. The pages exist, but the figures were not retrievable: [AI data security](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/ai-data-security-market-102310249.html), [AI data protection](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/ai-data-protection-market-227389738.html).
- I could not verify Gartner spending forecasts for AI TRiSM, and Mordor figures were not retrieved.

## 2. Evidence that employees paste sensitive data into AI tools; shadow AI

### Takeaway
Every major telemetry study finds sensitive data in a meaningful minority of AI interactions. Prompts are flagged at about 2.6–8.5% (Harmonic). File uploads are flagged at 22–40% (Harmonic, LayerX). About 35–40% of corporate data moved into AI tools is classed as sensitive (Cyberhaven). Personal or unmanaged accounts carry a large share of this traffic: 47% of genAI users (Netskope), 67–71% of AI usage (LayerX), and 72% of corporate-device users (Verizon). The trend is falling as enterprises roll out managed accounts, which shrinks the problem in big companies but not in small ones without managed tools.

### Cited Findings
**Harmonic Security (vendor; US/UK enterprise telemetry)**
- Q4 2024 (published Jan 2025): 8.5% of prompts to Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity contained potentially sensitive data. Of those, 45.8% risked exposing customer data — [Campus Technology, 22 Jan 2025](https://campustechnology.com/Articles/2025/01/22/Study-1-in-10-AI-Prompts-Could-Expose-Sensitive-Data.aspx)
- Q2 2025 (published 31 Jul 2025): 1M prompts and 20k files across 300 tools. 4.37% of prompts and 22% of files contained sensitive data. Source code was the most common type, and 72.6% of sensitive prompts were in ChatGPT — [Business Wire, 31 Jul 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250731456105/en)
- Q3 2025 (published 13 Nov 2025): over 3M prompts and uploads. 26.4% of file uploads contained sensitive data (up from 22%), and 12% of sensitive exposures came from personal or free accounts of ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude — [Business Wire, 13 Nov 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251113987633/en); [SecurityBrief UK](https://securitybrief.co.uk/story/sensitive-data-exposure-rises-with-employee-use-of-genai-tools)
- Full year 2025 (published Jan 2026): 22.4M prompts across six GenAI apps. 579,000 (2.6%) contained company-sensitive data. This is reported in search-result summaries; I did not fetch the primary source.

**Cyberhaven (vendor; customer telemetry)**
- 2025 AI Adoption & Risk Report (23 Apr 2025): 34.8% of corporate data employees put into AI tools is sensitive, up from 27.4% a year earlier and 10.7% two years earlier. By type, source code was 18.7% of sensitive data, R&D 17.1% and sales/marketing 10.7%. 83.8% of enterprise data going to AI tools went to platforms rated critical or high risk — [Cyberhaven press release](https://cyberhaven.com/press-releases/cyberhaven-report-majority-of-corporate-ai-tools-present-critical-data-security-risks); [BetaNews, 23 Apr 2025](https://betanews.com/2025/04/23/ai-tools-present-critical-data-risks-in-the-enterprise)
- 2026 AI Risk Report (covering 2025): 39.7% of data movements into AI tools involve sensitive data, and the average employee enters sensitive data into AI about once every three days. These figures come from search snippets; I did not read the PDF — [Cyberhaven AI Risk Report 2026 (PDF)](https://info.cyberhaven.com/hubfs/Webflow_Resources/Cyberhaven-AI-Risk-Report-2026.pdf). A professional-services edition exists: [PDF](https://info.cyberhaven.com/hubfs/Webflow_Resources/2026%20AI%20Risk%20Report%20-%20Professional%20Services.pdf)

**LayerX (vendor; browser-extension telemetry)**
- Enterprise AI & SaaS Data Security Report 2025 (Oct 2025): 77% of AI users copy and paste data into chatbot queries, and 22% of those pastes include PII or PCI data. 82% of pastes come from unmanaged personal accounts. 40% of files uploaded to GenAI contain PII or PCI data, and about 39% of those uploads come from non-corporate accounts. 67% of AI usage happens through unmanaged personal accounts. Coverage disagrees on whether the 77% base is all employees or AI users — [The Register, 7 Oct 2025](https://theregister.com/2025/10/07/gen_ai_shadow_it_secrets); [LayerX report PDF](https://go.layerxsecurity.com/hubfs/LayerX_Enterprise_AI_and_SaaS_Data_Security_Report.pdf); [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-employees-share-company-secrets-on-chatgpt/)
- Enterprise GenAI Security Report (Feb 2025): 71% of connections to GenAI tools used personal, non-corporate accounts — [The Register summary as above](https://theregister.com/2025/10/07/gen_ai_shadow_it_secrets)

**Netskope Threat Labs (vendor; Cloud & Threat Report 2026, published 5 Jan 2026, data Oct 2024–Oct 2025) (primary)**
- The average organisation logs about 223 genAI data-policy violations a month, involving 3% of its genAI users. The top quartile logs about 2,100 a month, involving 13% of genAI users. Violations roughly doubled year on year, while users tripled and prompts grew sixfold (median 3,000 to 18,000 prompts per organisation per month).
- By type, genAI violations were source code 42%, regulated data (personal, financial, health) 32% and intellectual property 16%.
- Users on personal AI apps fell from 78% to 47%, and users on organisation-managed accounts rose from 25% to 62%. Users switching between the two rose from 4% to 9%. 50% of organisations use DLP for genAI — [Netskope Threat Labs](https://threatlabs.netskope.com/reports/2026/cloud-and-threat-report-2026)
- Note: press coverage misreports "54% regulated data" as the genAI figure. In the primary report, 54% applies to *personal cloud app* violations, not genAI — compare [SecurityBrief UK](https://securitybrief.co.uk/story/generative-ai-drives-surge-in-workplace-data-breaches) with the primary page.

**Menlo Security (vendor; "How AI is Shaping the Modern Workspace", 4 Aug 2025)**
- Over 30 days (May–June 2025), Menlo telemetry recorded about 155,000 copy attempts and 313,000 paste attempts into GenAI.
- The report cites a TELUS Digital survey: 68% of employees use free-tier AI tools such as ChatGPT through personal accounts, and 57% of them enter sensitive company data. These are survey figures, not telemetry — [Menlo Security press release](https://www.menlosecurity.com/press-releases/menlo-securitys-2025-report-uncovers-68-surge-in-shadow-generative-ai-usage-in-the-modern-enterprise); [SiliconANGLE, 21 Aug 2025](https://siliconangle.com/2025/08/21/menlo-security-research-finds-use-of-shadow-ai-is-booming/)
- A 2024 Menlo study found 55% of GenAI inputs contained personally identifiable data — [Security Magazine](https://www.securitymagazine.com/articles/100400-55-of-generative-ai-inputs-comprised-personally-identifiable-data?v=preview) (2024 figure)

**Non-vendor / neutral sources**
- Verizon DBIR 2025: about 15% of employees routinely used GenAI on corporate devices. 72% of them used non-corporate email to sign in, and a further 17% used corporate email without SSO — [Kiteworks summary of DBIR 2025](https://www.kiteworks.com/third-party-risk/verizon-2025-dbir-third-party-risk-explosion/); [WaterISAC](https://www.waterisac.org/tlpclear-verizons-2025-data-breach-investigations-report-dbir)
- Verizon DBIR 2026 (May 2026): 45% of employees are now regular AI users (approved or not) on corporate systems, up from 15%. This comes from secondary coverage — [eSecurityPlanet](https://www.esecurityplanet.com/de/threats/2026-verizon-dbir-the-new-era-of-cyber-threats/)
- IBM Cost of a Data Breach 2025 (Jul 2025; Ponemon): 20% of organisations reported a breach involving shadow AI. High shadow-AI use added about USD 670k to average breach cost (USD 4.63M vs 3.96M). Shadow-AI incidents compromised more PII (65%) and IP (40%). Only 37% of organisations have policies to manage AI or detect shadow AI — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-ai-breach-risks-rise-as-governance-lags/); [Baker Donelson, 10 key insights](https://www.bakerdonelson.com/ten-key-insights-from-ibms-cost-of-a-data-breach-report-2025)
- Samsung incident (2023): Economist Korea reported three leaks in the semiconductor division on 30 Mar 2023, two involving source code and one involving meeting notes, all pasted into ChatGPT. A memo dated 2 May 2023 banned generative AI on company devices and networks and told staff not to submit company or personal data on personal devices, on pain of dismissal — [Bloomberg, 2 May 2023](https://bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-05-02/samsung-bans-chatgpt-and-other-generative-ai-use-by-staff-after-leak); [Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/amp/technology/tech-news/samsung-bans-employees-from-using-ai-after-spotting-chatgpt-data-leak-123050200197_1.html) (2023)

### Inferences
- A defensible headline is: "1 in 40 to 1 in 12 prompts, and 1 in 5 to 2 in 5 file uploads, contain sensitive data (Harmonic, LayerX, 2025)". Vendor definitions of "sensitive" are broad and include source code, so for Ghost Agent's PII and health focus the relevant slice is smaller. Netskope's "regulated data 32% of genAI violations" is the best proxy.
- The fall in personal-account use (Netskope 78% to 47%) happens because large enterprises roll out ChatGPT Enterprise or Copilot. Small clinics, schools and firms without IT mostly lack managed accounts, so their staff stay on free consumer tiers. The free tiers are exactly where training-use defaults and no DPA apply. This is a positioning argument for Ghost Agent, but an inference.
- All behavioural evidence comes from large US/UK enterprises. No study measures SMBs or Nordic users directly.

### Gaps
- No telemetry study segmented by company size (SMB vs enterprise) or by country (Nordics).
- No study isolating *health data* pasting rates. Netskope's "regulated data" bundles personal, financial and health data.
- I did not fetch the primary PDFs for Harmonic full-year 2025, Cyberhaven 2026 or LayerX 2025, so denominators should be checked before quoting.

## 3. AI chat adoption: Norwegian/Nordic SMBs, public sector, individuals, teachers, health workers

### Takeaway
Norway: 3 in 10 enterprises with 10+ employees used AI in 2025, up from 1 in 10 in 2023. 54% of 16–79-year-olds used generative AI in Q2 2025, up from 36% in 2024. About 54–55% of public organisations used AI in 2025, rising to 66% in 2026. Data and privacy concerns hold back 4 in 10 non-adopting firms in Norway and 49% in the EU. Denmark (42%) and Finland (38%) lead EU enterprise AI adoption. Small firms lag badly: 17% EU-wide in the 10–49 class. Individuals use AI far more than their employers sanction it.

### Cited Findings
**Norway: enterprises (SSB)**
- 2025: "3 av 10 norske foretak med minst ti sysselsatte" used one or more AI technologies, up from 2 in 10 in 2024 and 1 in 10 in 2023 (about 10 percentage points of growth a year). Enterprises with 100+ employees rose from 27% to 58% (2023 to 2025). Enterprises with under 50 employees "more than tripled" AI use since 2023 from a very low base; no exact figure is given in the text. SSB names generative AI as the main driver. Of the 70% not using AI, 77% cite lack of competence, and 4 in 10 cite concern about data and privacy and unclear legal consequences — [SSB, 24 Sep 2025 (primary)](https://www.ssb.no/teknologi-og-innovasjon/informasjons-og-kommunikasjonsteknologi-ikt/statistikk/bruk-av-ikt-i-naeringslivet/artikler/bruken-av-ki-har-skutt-fart-det-siste-aret). StatBank table 13272 holds the non-use reasons.

**EU/Nordics: enterprises (Eurostat)**
- 2025: 19.95% of EU enterprises (10+ employees) used AI, up 6.47 percentage points from 2024. By size: small (10–49) 17.00%, medium (50–249) 30.36%, large (250+) 55.03%. Denmark is highest at 42.03% (up 14.45 pp), followed by Finland at 37.82% (up 13.45 pp) and Sweden at 35.04%. Norway appears in a figure, but its value is not stated in the text. 8.76% of EU enterprises use natural-language or code-generation AI (31.68% of large ones).
- Reasons for not using AI, among enterprises that had considered it: lack of expertise 70.89%, unclear legal consequences 52.52%, **concerns about violating data protection and privacy 48.83%** — [Eurostat Statistics Explained, data extracted Dec 2025 (primary)](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Use_of_artificial_intelligence_in_enterprises). The next update is scheduled for 15 Dec 2026.

**Norway: public sector and municipalities**
- IT i praksis 2025 (Rambøll with Digdir, KS, TEK-Norge and NTNU; Sep 2025): over half of public organisations use AI, with 55% reported for 2025. Only 1 in 4 converts the efficiency gains into real cost savings. Over 4 in 5 state agencies and municipalities say lack of insight into use cases is a barrier — [NTB press release, IT i praksis 2025](https://kommunikasjon.ntb.no/pressemelding/18662174/it-i-praksis-2025-over-halvparten-av-offentlige-virksomheter-bruker-na-kunstig-intelligens-men-omstillingen-uteblir); [Rambøll IT i praksis 2025](https://www.ramboll.com/no-no/it-i-praksis-2025)
- The 2025 sample was 273 public organisations: 188 municipalities and 85 state or county bodies — [NTNU NIKT paper "Bruk av KI i norsk offentlig sektor: status 2025"](https://www.ntnu.no/ojs/index.php/nikt/article/view/6548)
- IT i praksis 2026: 66% of public organisations use AI, up from 54% in 2025 and 14% in 2021. Note the small inconsistency: 54% vs 55% for 2025 — [KS, IT i praksis 2026 full report (PDF)](https://www.ks.no/contentassets/298ce53af7eb4a6894c87e32bdfe0f03/it-i-praksis-2026-samlet-rapport.pdf). A KS page headline reads "Kunstig intelligens brukes av 7 av 10 offentlige virksomheter" — [KS](https://www.ks.no/fagomrader/digitalisering/kunstig-intelligens-ki/it-i-praksis-2025-kunstig-intelligens-brukes-av-7-av-10-offentlige-virksomheter/). The page returned 403, so I could not confirm the year and base.
- Municipal planners describe "støtteverktøy som Copilot/ChatGPT" as current practice — [Menon Economics / Winther-Larsen for KDD, 2025](https://www.regjeringen.no/contentassets/28ce4a8c52f44897a65351ce15528e69/menon_wintherlarsen.pdf)
- KS has a 2025 plan for AI use in the municipal sector (policy, not survey data) — [KS](https://www.ks.no/fagomrader/digitalisering/kunstig-intelligens-ki/plan-for-bruk-av-kunstig-intelligens-i-kommunal-sektor-2025/)

**Norway: individuals**
- SSB (Q2 2025, ages 16–79, last 3 months): 54% used generative AI, up from 36% in 2024. Men 58%, women 49%. Ages 16–24 about 80%, 25–34 about 70%, 35–44 nearly 70% (up 30 pp), 45–54 56% (up from 35%) — [SSB, 24 Sep 2025 (primary)](https://www.ssb.no/teknologi-og-innovasjon/informasjons-og-kommunikasjonsteknologi-ikt/statistikk/bruk-av-ikt-i-naeringslivet/artikler/bruken-av-ki-har-skutt-fart-det-siste-aret); see also [SSB "7 av 10 unge bruker KI"](https://www.ssb.no/teknologi-og-innovasjon/informasjons-og-kommunikasjonsteknologi-ikt/statistikk/bruk-av-ikt-i-husholdningene/artikler/7-av-10-unge-bruker-ki)
- Reuters Institute / Oxford, Generative AI and News Report 2026 (Norway included for the first time): a Norwegian press release reports that 57% of Norwegians use an AI service such as ChatGPT weekly, 25% daily, and 44% use ChatGPT weekly. It also says Norwegians are top for paying for AI services. I took these from search snippets and did not verify them on the primary page — [NTB press release "Nordmenn på topp i betaling for KI-tjenester, viser ny Oxford-studie"](https://kommunikasjon.ntb.no/pressemelding/19121552/nordmenn-pa-topp-i-betaling-for-ki-tjenester-viser-ny-oxford-studie?lang=no); [Reuters Institute 2026 report](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/generative-ai-and-news-report-2026-standalone-tools-information-infrastructure). This is from a search snippet; I could not parse the PDF — [KPMG AI-undersøkelsen 2025 (PDF)](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/no/pdf/ai/ai-undersokelsen-2025.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf); [KPMG page](https://kpmg.com/no/nb/innsikt/teknologi-og-kunstig-intelligens/kpmgs-ai-undersokelse.html)

### Inferences
- There is a large "use gap" in Norway. 54% of adults use GenAI (SSB 2025), while only about 30% of 10+ employee firms report using AI, and fewer small ones. Many employees in small organisations are therefore likely using personal GenAI accounts for work without an organisational tool or policy. This is the shadow-AI pattern seen in the vendor data, but inferred, not measured, for Norway.
- Privacy concern is a top-3 adoption barrier both in Norway (4 in 10 non-adopters) and the EU (48.8%). A tool that makes GenAI use "safe" could be sold as an *adoption enabler*, not only as protection.
- Denmark and Finland have higher enterprise AI adoption than the EU and are natural second markets after Norway.

### Gaps
- No exact Norwegian AI-use share for the 10–49 size class or by industry (health, education, legal/accounting). The data is in SSB StatBank (tables 13272 and others) but not stated in article text.
- Norway's Eurostat 2025 value was not extracted (it is in the isoc_eb_ai dataset).
- No KS survey isolating municipal use of ChatGPT or Copilot was found. IT i praksis covers public organisations overall.
- No Medietilsynet or Norstat population figure for ChatGPT specifically was found. The Reuters/Oxford Norway figure comes from a press release and is unverified.
- No Norwegian data on teachers' or health workers' GenAI use or on what data they paste.

## 4. Breach and fine statistics relevant to small organisations

### Takeaway
Norway's Datatilsynet fines rarely: 2 fines in 2025, down from 5 in 2024. Complaints doubled to 1,848, and one of the two fines hit a municipality. Across the EU, healthcare GDPR fines are growing (265 fines, about EUR 32.3M in total, most for inadequate security measures). SMBs are disproportionately hit by ransomware (88% of SMB breaches, DBIR 2025). Shadow AI adds about USD 670k to breach costs, though that figure is enterprise-scale.

### Cited Findings
- **Datatilsynet 2025** (from the ministry's report to Stortinget): 36 decisions on corrective measures or fines in 2025 (44 in 2024). Fines were issued in only 2 cases, one to a municipality after an inspection of tracking tools and one to a larger private company, down from 5 in 2024. Complaints from individuals rose from 902 (2024) to 1,848 (2025). 71 of 374 decisions were appealed — [Meld. St. 13 (2025–2026), Digitaliserings- og forvaltningsdepartementet](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/dokumenter/meld.-st.-13-20252026/id3170563/?ch=1)
- **Norwegian precedents involving health or public-sector data:** NAV fined NOK 20M — [Security World Market](https://www.securityworldmarket.com/no/Nyheter/Bedriftsnyheter/vedtak-om-gebyr-og-palegg-til-nav); Oslo kommune fined NOK 400,000 for publishing sensitive personal data, including health data, in eInnsyn — [Legiscope overview](https://www.legiscope.com/blog/datatilsynet-overtredelsesgebyr-2025.html); Sykehuset Innlandet accepted an NOK 800,000 fine in 2018 — [Sykehuset Innlandet board document (2018)](https://www.sykehuset-innlandet.no/49eba6/siteassets/styret/2018/2018-06/049-2018-vedtak-om-overtredelsesgebyr-fra-datatilsynet.pdf)
- **EU healthcare GDPR fines (CMS Enforcement Tracker, life sciences & healthcare chapter, 2026 edition):** 265 fines across 27 countries, about EUR 32.3M in total (+44 fines vs the 2025 edition). Inadequate technical and organisational measures is the top cause (100 fines, about EUR 22.8M). Italy issued 95 fines, Spain 30 and Germany 29. The largest was EUR 3.5M (UK) — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/deu/publication/GDPR-Enforcement-Tracker-Report/life-science-healthcare). The whole tracker held 2,560 fines as of 1 Mar 2025 — [CMS ETR May 2025](https://cms.law/en/media/international/files/publications/publications/gdpr-enforcement-tracker-report-may-2025)
- **Verizon DBIR 2025:** ransomware was present in 88% of SMB breaches vs 39% at larger organisations (44% overall) — [Prey Project summary of DBIR 2025](https://preyproject.com/blog/verizon-dbir-data-breach-report); [WaterISAC](https://www.waterisac.org/tlpclear-verizons-2025-data-breach-investigations-report-dbir). DBIR 2026 (May 2026) says SMBs were about 96% of ransomware victims where size was known — [eSecurityPlanet](https://www.esecurityplanet.com/de/threats/2026-verizon-dbir-the-new-era-of-cyber-threats/)
- **IBM Cost of a Data Breach 2025:** shadow-AI premium of about USD 670k; US average cost USD 10.22M (a record); 20% of organisations had a shadow-AI breach; 37% have AI governance policies — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-ai-breach-risks-rise-as-governance-lags/); [Baker Donelson](https://www.bakerdonelson.com/ten-key-insights-from-ibms-cost-of-a-data-breach-report-2025)

### Inferences
- In Norway, the realistic risk for a small clinic or school is not a fine. Datatilsynet issued only 2 fines in 2025. The real risks are breach notification, reputational damage, a complaint (complaints are rising fast) and possible orders. The sales message should lean on duty of care and GDPR compliance ("art. 32 appropriate measures"), not on fear of fines.
- Inadequate technical and organisational measures is the most-fined healthcare category in the EU. A cheap technical control maps directly onto it.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve Datatilsynet's own 2025 annual report, so there is no figure for breach notifications (avviksmeldinger), the health-sector share, or any GenAI-related breach cases in Norway.
- No Norwegian decision concerning a small clinic or dental practice was found.
- No SMB-specific average breach cost (IBM reports by industry and region, not by small size class in the material reviewed). No Nordic cost figure.
- No documented GDPR fine yet for an employee pasting personal data into ChatGPT (for comparison, Italy's Garante fined OpenAI itself; not researched here).
